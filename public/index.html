How this version works

Price data comes from a live Takealot data API. Your server keeps the watchlist and price snapshots locally. Nothing is presented as a fake/demo price. The server can re-check watched items on a schedule while it is running.
PriceWatch is an independent price-tracking interface. Prices and stock can change at the retailer. Always verify the final price on the retailer before buying.
